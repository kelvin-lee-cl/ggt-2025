Capstone 2

Create your own WhatsApp stickers or memes

Use AI (and simple design steps) to make fun, shareable visuals that stay respectful and original.

Objective

Produce a small set of stickers or meme-style images (and optionally a short GIF) that share a clear theme. When you are ready, use the submission page to upload one representative image and a written explanation of how AI supported your workflow (ideas, drafts, edits, or exports).

Project outline
  • Choose a theme (club, inside joke, mascot—keep it kind and appropriate).
  • Create visuals with AI assistance; crop or compose as needed for sticker sizes.
  • Package exports (PNG/WebP as required) for your full set.
  • Submit online: one preview image plus your explanation of how AI helped.

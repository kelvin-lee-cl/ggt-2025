Capstone 1

Create your own AI writing tutor

Using any chatbot you can access (Perplexity, Poe, ChatGPT, etc.), configure a writing tutor that coaches people through planning, drafting, or revising—not one that simply writes the piece for them.

Objective

Choose your own audience and writing situation (e.g. school essay, story, professional email, blog). Show how you instructed the AI to guide users toward clearer structure, stronger word choices, and their own voice—while avoiding shortcuts like pasting a finished essay.

Project outline
  • Pick one writing situation and decide who the tutor is for (you define the audience).
  • Set the tutor’s behaviour: tone, coaching style, and what it must not do (e.g. write full assignments).
  • For inspiration or reference, review the site’s Text generator—notice how prompts and constraints shape what the model does.
  • Publish your tutor so it has a shareable link from the platform (bot page, custom GPT, Poe bot, etc.).
  • Submit only that link using the submission page below.
